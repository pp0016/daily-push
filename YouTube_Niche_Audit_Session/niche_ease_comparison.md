## 1. 🧮 Math Education (Brain Station Advanced Style)

### Difficulty: ⭐ EASIEST

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Tools Needed** | Manim (free Python library), PowerPoint, or Canva |
| **Voice** | TTS works fine (Byte Sized proves this) |
| **Face** | No face needed — fully faceless |
| **Cost** | **$0** — all tools are free |
| **Production Time** | ~2-4 hours per video |
| **Skills** | Basic math (calculus, algebra, geometry) |
| **Upload Pace** | 2-3 per week |

### NexLev Revenue Data (@BrainStationAdvanced)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **RPM** | $1.72 (category avg $2.00) |
| **Monthly Revenue** | $3,065 |
| **Lifetime Earnings** | ~$51,430 in 1.75 years |
| **Per Year** | ~$29,389/year (avg) → $36,780/year (current rate) |
| **Per Video** | $161.22 |
| **Per Month (avg)** | $2,449 |
| **Tier 1 Traffic** | 70.3% (US+UK+CA+AU) |

### Why It's the EASIEST

1. **No animation skill needed** — PowerPoint slides with equations + step-by-step solutions
2. **No voice needed** — TTS or even just text-on-screen works
3. **No editing skill** — Record screen while solving math problems
4. **Infinite content ideas** — Every math topic from algebra to calculus = a video
5. **Evergreen search traffic** — "How to solve integrals" gets searched forever
6. **India-based creator earning Tier 1 money** — 70.3% traffic from US/UK/CA/AU

### ⚠️ Downsides

- **Lowest RPM** of all niches ($1.72) — math doesn't attract premium advertisers
- **Needs math knowledge** — you need to understand calculus/trigonometry/algebra
- **Revenue ceiling** is lower — $3K/month at 1.78M monthly views
- **Audience is old** — 64.5% are 45+ (not necessarily bad, but niche)
