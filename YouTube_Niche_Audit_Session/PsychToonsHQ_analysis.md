## 10. Should You Replicate PsychToons?

### ✅ Pros
- Psychology is a massive evergreen niche
- Extremely low competition (only 3 similar channels, all under 12K subs)
- The title formula ("Psychology of X: [Bold Claim]") is proven viral
- Faceless, no equipment needed
- **\$67K/year projected** at current growth

### ❌ Cons
- **Custom illustrations required** — this is NOT PowerPoint animation
- **\$1.69 RPM** is the lowest of all 10 channels analyzed
- **\$98/video** revenue — much lower than Byte Sized (\$4,678/video) or Rock in Space (\$5,671/video)
- Need animation software skills (After Effects, Procreate, or similar)
- 5 uploads/week = very demanding schedule
- Revenue is driven by VOLUME not efficiency

### Verdict

> [!CAUTION]
> **PsychToons is NOT easy to replicate.** The animations are custom-illustrated, not template-based. Each video requires: (1) psychology research + scriptwriting, (2) custom character illustrations, (3) animation of those illustrations, (4) voiceover. At 5 videos/week, this is likely a small team, not a solo creator.
>
> Compare with **Math Education** (Brain Station) which needs only PowerPoint + equations, or **Tech Explainers** (Byte Sized) which needs only icon grids + TTS. PsychToons' visual quality is significantly higher and harder to achieve.
