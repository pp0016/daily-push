## 5. Key Insights

### 🎯 The Unusual Audience Profile
This is one of the most unusual demographic profiles you'll see on YouTube. **64.5% of viewers are 45+**, and nearly a quarter (23.4%) are **65 or older**. This is NOT a student channel — these are adults revisiting math out of curiosity or helping their children/grandchildren. The 83.4% male skew is typical for math/engineering content.

### 💰 Revenue Efficiency
At \$161 revenue per video and 93.6K views per video, this channel has strong per-video efficiency for the math niche. The pure long-form strategy avoids the Shorts RPM trap (\$0.038 RPM for Shorts vs \$1.72 for long-form — a **45x difference**).

### 📈 Growth Trajectory
460K subs in 21 months = **~21,900 subs/month** average. The channel is clearly in a growth phase with current monthly revenue 25% above the lifetime average. The "5-minute math explainer" format has strong evergreen search potential.

### 🌍 Geographic Arbitrage
An India-based creator earning at near-Tier-1 RPM rates by creating English content that attracts 70.3% Tier 1 traffic. The \$3,065/month (\$36,780/year annualized) is exceptional income for an Indian creator — roughly equivalent to a senior software engineer salary in many Indian cities.

### ⚠️ RPM Ceiling
The \$1.72 RPM is below category average (\$2.00) due to the 29.7% India traffic. Math/education doesn't attract premium advertisers the way finance (\$8-15 RPM) or tech (\$4-8 RPM) does. Growth in revenue will likely come from view volume rather than RPM increases.
