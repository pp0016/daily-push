## 7. Key Insight

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### The Indian Diaspora Revenue Machine
>
> FactTechz proves that **Hindi content can earn at near-Western RPMs** if the audience is the Indian diaspora (NRIs in US/UK/Canada/Australia). Key factors:
>
> 1. **\$4.31 RPM** — higher than most English channels we analyzed (Brain Station \$1.72, PsychToons \$1.69, MARINARA \$1.98)
> 2. **45.8% US traffic** — NRIs watching Hindi content pay American ad rates
> 3. **287M monthly views** — absolutely massive reach
> 4. **99% of revenue from long-form** — Shorts are a views magnet but revenue irrelevant
> 5. **10 years of consistency** — 2,500 videos, built slowly from 2016
>
> **This is NOT replicable quickly.** This took a decade of daily Hindi content, a recognizable face, and a loyal NRI audience. But it proves that Hindi + diaspora targeting can be as profitable as English content.
