# YouTube Niche Strategy: Dual-Channel Approach
**Date:** July 16, 2026 (Logged for GitHub Daily Push)

## The Strategy
After exhaustive research into Tier 1 English niches, we identified that the **still images + Ken Burns + AI voiceover** format is heavily rejected in Horror, but has a proven, uncrowded lane in **Dark History**.

### Channel 1: Hindi Audience (Velocity/Cashflow)
*   **Niche:** IPS / UPSC Fictional Emotional Drama
*   **Format:** AI Images + ElevenLabs Hindi + Remotion captions
*   **Target:** ?50K-1L/month via extreme view velocity (proven by Nova Story CSV data).
*   **First Video:** IPS/UPSC drama format.

### Channel 2: English Tier 1 Audience (High RPM)
*   **Niche:** Dark History / Disturbing Historical Events
*   **Format:** Cinematic AI Photorealistic Scene Reconstructions + ElevenLabs English + Ken Burns
*   **Target:** $4K-$5K/month via high RPM ($7-$16) × moderate views.
*   **The "Gap":** Filling the space between archival photos (Forgotten Lives) and full animation (Al Muqaddimah) with cinematic AI reconstructions (Nano Banana 2).
*   **First Video:** Pont-Saint-Esprit mass poisoning ("This Town Went Insane Overnight — Nobody Knows Why"). Zero copyright risk, public domain historical event, perfect for AI visuals.

## Key Insights from Research
1.  **Why Not Horror?** Reddit horror communities actively filter for "human narration only." The audience rejects AI voice because the voice *is* the product in horror.
2.  **Why Not Unsolved Mysteries?** Credibility requires real victim photos and location shots. A 100% AI-generated missing persons case feels wrong to the audience.
3.  **Why Not Reddit Drama?** Saturated and heavily penalized by YouTube's "inauthentic content" sweeps.
4.  **Why Not "What If"?** The leaders use animation and motion graphics, which doesn't fit the AI image + Ken Burns stack.
