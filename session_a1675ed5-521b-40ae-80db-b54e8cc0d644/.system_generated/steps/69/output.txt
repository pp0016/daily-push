### **Key Insights for 2026**

*   **The AI Split:** If you need to produce videos quickly, **AI-first tools** (like Animaker's latest updates or specialized AI video agents) are now the standard. They allow you to input a script, document, or prompt, and the software handles the scene generation, timing, and asset placement automatically.
*   **Manual Control:** If your priority is "fine-grained" artistic control—where you must dictate every specific drawing stroke or motion—traditional tools like **VideoScribe** or **Doodly** remain the industry standard.
*   **Pricing Models:** 
    *   **Doodly** remains popular for its "one-time purchase" model, which can be more cost-effective than monthly subscriptions if you plan to create videos over a long period.
    *   **Animaker** and others operate on subscription models, which are generally better if you require constant access to updated AI features, cloud assets, and high-quality 4K exports.

### **Which one to choose?**
*   **Choose Animaker** if you want a browser-based, all-in-one solution that balances manual customization with modern AI prompt-based generation.
